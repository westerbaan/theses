# Lean formalization of the theses

A Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization of the two PhD theses in this repository:

* **Thesis A** — Abraham Westerbaan, *The Category of Von Neumann Algebras*
  ([arXiv:1804.02203](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02203)): `cstar.tex`,
  `vn.tex`, `proc.tex`, solutions and errata in `asols.tex`.
* **Thesis B** — Bas Westerbaan, *Dagger and Dilation in the Category of Von
  Neumann Algebras* ([arXiv:1803.01911](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.01911)):
  `dils.tex`, `eff.tex`, solutions in `bsols.tex`, errata in `berr.tex`.

Essentially every lemma, proposition, theorem, corollary and exercise of both
theses is stated, and all but nine are proved.  The nine are unproved because
the printed statement is false or waits on an author ruling; each has a row in
`docs/why-open.csv`, and `Theses/AxiomCheck.lean` verifies that nothing else
depends on them.  Every statement has been audited against its source
(`docs/STATEMENT-AUDIT.md`, rows in `docs/audit/*.csv`), and every proof is
classed by whether it follows the thesis's own argument.

## Where to look

| want | read |
|---|---|
| what the authors are being asked to rule on | `docs/DECISIONS.md` (the short form), `QUESTIONS.md` (the full form) |
| defects found in the theses | `ERRATA.md` |
| one box per statement, coloured by status | `docs/sorry-map.html` (generated by `scripts/sorry_map.py`) |
| why a statement is unproved | `docs/why-open.csv` |
| how a declaration cites its point, and how to find a solution | `CONVENTIONS.md` |
| the audit schema and verdict vocabulary | `docs/STATEMENT-AUDIT.md` |
| what happened, session by session | `PROVING-LOG.md` (append-only; never rewritten) |
| per-chapter surveys and the dead-code sweep | `docs/*-survey.md`, `docs/DEAD-LIMBS.md`, `docs/COMMUTATION-THEOREM.md` |

## Layout

```
Theses/
  Common.lean            -- Kadison-style von Neumann algebra class, np-functionals,
                         --   nmiu/ncp/ncpsu maps, effects
  AxiomCheck.lean        -- #sorry_leaks over the whole tree
  A/CStar/               -- cstar.tex: Basic, Positive, Representation, Matrices, TowardsVN
  A/VN/                  -- vn.tex: Basic, Projections, Completeness, Division,
                         --   NormalFunctionals, BaX; and the Tomita–Takesaki development
                         --   (StandardSubspace, Tomita*, Modular*, CommutationTomita)
  A/Proc/                -- proc.tex: Measurement, Tensor, QuantumLambda, Duplicators,
                         --   Compression, CornerTensor, TensorTransport; and the
                         --   commutation theorem (Commutation*)
  B/Dils/                -- dils.tex: Stinespring, HilbertModules, SelfDualCompletion,
                         --   Paschke, Kaplansky, SelfDual, Pure
  B/Eff/                 -- eff.tex: EffectAlgebras, Effectus, WStarCat, StatesPredicates,
                         --   Quotients, DiamondAmp, Dagger, Comparisons, VNExamples
```

Every declaration's doc comment starts with the thesis's point number in bold
(**34V** = parsec 34, point V, the numbers in the margins of the printed
theses), then the LaTeX label, the source `file:line`, and the point's tag.
Grepping for `**34V**` finds the formalization of point 34V.  The Tomita–Takesaki
and commutation-theorem files are mostly untagged: they are machinery the
theses cite from the literature, built here because Mathlib lacks it.

## Building and checking

Mathlib is fetched with `lake exe cache get`.  **Do not run `lake build` or a
language server on the reference box**: one `lean` process peaks at 7.2 GB and
the box has 14 GB and no swap, so two compiles do not fit.  Instead:

```
scripts/lean1.sh Theses/A/VN/Basic.lean   # compile one file, serialised through a lock
scripts/refresh_oleans.py --write         # rebuild stale .oleans in dependency order
scripts/lean1.sh Theses/AxiomCheck.lean   # confirm nothing depends on a sorry
scripts/check_all.py                      # every bookkeeping check, one summary
```

`check_all.py` runs the checkers in `scripts/`: citations against the LaTeX
(`cite_check`), `File.lean:N` references against declarations
(`lean_line_check`), the audit CSVs against the tree (`audit_check`),
`ERRATA.md`, `QUESTIONS.md`, `DEAD-LIMBS.md` and `docs/*.md §N` cross-references
against what they cite, doc-comment settings against binders
(`vn_setting_check`), olean staleness, and the verdict reader of `sorry_map.py`
against itself.  `kaplansky_witness.py` recomputes the 158V counterexample in
exact arithmetic.

## Rules of the tree

* A statement is never changed without an author ruling.  Two exceptions,
  both logged in `PROVING-LOG.md`: our own mis-transcription of the printed
  text, and — by the ruling of 2026-09-04 on `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.1 — a
  change that brings a statement *closer to the printed one*, made only inside
  a dedicated statement-alignment pass (`docs/audit/STATEMENT-BRIEF.md`), never
  as a side effect of another job.
* A `sorry` is never added.  A statement that turns out false as printed keeps
  its `sorry` as documentation, gets a machine-checked counterexample beside it
  where one is possible, and a row in `ERRATA.md`.
* An erratum is filed only if a *reader* of the thesis would stumble, not
  merely a checker.
* `PROVING-LOG.md` and the block-quoted session entries in the surveys are
  records of what was true when written and are not rewritten.
