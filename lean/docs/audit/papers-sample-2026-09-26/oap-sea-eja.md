# Re-audit of the sampled OAP, SEA and EJA rows (2026-09-26)

Read-only re-grade of the 48 sampled rows from papers-oap.csv, papers-sea.csv and papers-eja.csv (docs/audit/PAPERS-SAMPLE-2026-09-26.md), each checked against the printed point (and its proof) and the named Lean. Seven of the defects share one pattern. The OAP phase-1 rows grade `route` where the print gives no proof (a Definition's or Example's side claims: "Observe that…", "Note that…", "Conversely…"). By the vocabulary (docs/STATEMENT-AUDIT.md: `none` means "the thesis gives no proof (Definition, Example, or a bare citation)"; Papers/README.md: "A proof of ours for a point the paper only cites is `none`"), these should be `none`. Several of the rows' own notes say "No proof printed". The same pattern appears in unsampled rows too (papers-oap.csv:6,7,14,16,17,28,30,46,54,60,119). The sibling SIG re-audit graded the same situation UNSURE (papers-sig.csv:18); here it is DEFECT because both documents state the rule outright.
