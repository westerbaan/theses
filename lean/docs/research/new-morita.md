# Morita theory and the bicategory of bimodules

Research note, 2026-09-12. No Lean; conjectures with sketches, unaudited. Neither
thesis mentions Morita or the interior tensor; dils 1640–1670 is the *exterior* one.

Setting. `W*Corr` is the bicategory with objects von Neumann algebras, 1-cells
`𝒜 ⇸ ℬ` self-dual Hilbert `ℬ`-modules `X` with an nmiu `ρ_X : 𝒜 → ℬᵃ(X)`
(right-module convention of dils.tex), 2-cells bounded bimodule maps (adjointable
by dils 148-ish `hilbmod-adjoint-exists`), composition the interior tensor
`X ⊗_ℬ Y` := self-dual completion (150II) of `X ⊙ Y` with
`⟨x⊗y, x'⊗y'⟩ = ⟨y, ⟨x,x'⟩y'⟩`, `𝒜` acting by `ρ_X(a) ⊗ 1`. Unit `𝒜 ⇸ 𝒜` is
`𝒜` itself. Corner picture (`modules-as-corners.md`): `X = (1−e)Le`, `eLe = ℬ`.
152X says exactly that each hom-category `W*Corr(𝒜,ℬ)` is a W*-category
(Ghez–Lima–Roberts): `ℬᵃ(X) = End(X)` is a von Neumann algebra.

## (i) Paschke dilation = the initial pointed correspondence; composition is colax

**S1 (Paschke module is initial).** For ncp `φ : 𝒜 → ℬ` let `PCorr(φ)` have objects
`(X, ξ)`, `X : 𝒜 ⇸ ℬ`, `ξ ∈ X`, `⟨ξ, ρ_X(a)ξ⟩ = φ(a)` for all `a`; morphisms bounded
bimodule maps `T` with `Tξ = η`. Then `(E_φ, e_φ) := (𝒜 ⊗_φ ℬ, 1⊗1)` is *initial*,
the unique morphism `E_φ → X` is automatically isometric, and it is an iso iff `ξ`
is cyclic (`𝒜ξℬ` ultranorm dense). *Sketch.* `B(a,b) := ρ_X(a)ξb` is φ-compatible
(inner products match by definition), so 158/`existence-paschke` gives the unique
module map with `a⊗b ↦ ρ_X(a)ξb`; bimodularity and isometry hold on the dense span.
This is KSGNS (Lance ch. 5) in the self-dual setting; Skeide calls it the
GNS-correspondence of φ. Not in dils.tex as a statement, but all inputs are.

**S2 (algebra-level universal property from S1).** Given `(𝒫', ρ', h')` with
`h'∘ρ' = φ`, `(E_{h'}, e_{h'})` with `𝒜` acting through `ρ'` is an object of
`PCorr(φ)`; let `W : E_φ → E_{h'}` be the S1 map (an isometry). Then the mediating
map of def-paschke 1057/20 is **compression along W**: `σ(t) = W* ρ_{h'}(t) W`.
So the thesis's dilation is "initial pointed 1-cell, then take `End`". Refutation
risk: low — uniqueness of σ is dils 3727; only the identification is new.
Test: for `h' = h_p` (paschke-corner 6245) `W` is the inclusion `𝒜p ↪ 𝒜` — matches.

**S3 (157IV is the 2-endomorphism algebra).** `ρ(𝒜)' ∩ ℬᵃ(E_φ) = End_{W*Corr}(E_φ)`,
so paschke-correspondence reads: *the poset `[0,φ]_ncp` is the positive unit ball
of the von Neumann algebra of 2-endomorphisms of the 1-cell `E_φ`*, `T ↦ ⟨e, T ρ(·) e⟩`.
The "order correspondence" axiom of eff 2230 is thus the axiom "2-cells of the
dilation see the ncp-order". Bicategorical corollaries for free: `[0,φ] ≅ [0,φ']`
whenever `E_φ ≅ E_φ'` as 1-cells (e.g. `φ' = φ∘θ`, θ an automorphism).

**S4 (composition of dilations is fusion — laxly).** For `𝒜 →φ ℬ →ψ 𝒞` there is
a canonical isometric bimodule map
    `E_{ψ∘φ} ↪ E_φ ⊗_ℬ E_ψ`,  `a ⊗ c ↦ (a ⊗ 1) ⊗ (1 ⊗ c)`,  `e_{ψ∘φ} ↦ e_φ ⊗ e_ψ`,
iso iff `e_φ ⊗ e_ψ` is cyclic. It **is** an iso when ψ is nmiu (`E_ψ ≅ 𝒞`,
generators `a e_φ ψ(b) c` exhaust) and when φ is an automorphism; it is **not** an
iso in general, not even for φ nmiu. Counterexample: `𝒜 = ℂ`, `φ(1) = b ∈ ℬ⁺`,
`ψ = ω` a normal state. `E_φ ≅ ⌈b⌉ℬ` with `e_φ = √b`, `E_ω = H_ω` (GNS), so
`E_φ ⊗_ℬ E_ω = ⌈b⌉H_ω` while `E_{ω∘φ} = ℂ·√b Ω_ω`; proper whenever
`dim ⌈b⌉H_ω > 1`. Even `φ` unital nmiu `ℂ → ℬ` gives `ℂ ↪ H_ω`. So *Paschke is a
colax functor* from ncp maps into `W*Corr` with isometric comparison 2-cells, and
`Paschke(ψ∘φ)` is the sub-1-cell of the fusion generated by the tensor of the
points. This is the Bhat–Skeide construction (product systems from CP semigroups:
`E_{s+t} ⊆ E_s ⊗ E_t`, then inductive limit) — the composition statement dils.tex
lacks. At the algebra level: `ℬᵃ(E_φ ⊗ E_ψ)` with `ρ_φ ⊗ 1` and `T ↦ ⟨e_φ⊗e_ψ, T(e_φ⊗e_ψ)⟩`
is a (non-Paschke) dilation of `ψ∘φ`, and the mediating map to the Paschke one is
compression along the S4 isometry, as in S2.

**Gap.** dils.tex has no interior tensor product; via the corner picture `X ⊗_ℬ Y`
is a corner of the amalgamated linking algebra, and normality of `ρ_X ⊗ 1` needs a
150II-style density argument (163II's exterior proof transfers).

## (ii) Effectus-theoretic Morita equivalence

Needed vocabulary in a †-effectus `C`: sharp predicates `SPred(A)`, comprehension
`{A|p} → A`, centre `Z(A) ⊆ SPred(A)` (the object-wise centre of `support-order.md`:
`z` sharp with `asrt_z ∘ asrt_q = asrt_q ∘ asrt_z` for all `q`; in `vNᵒᵖ` this is
`z` central since it must commute with all projections), and **full**: `p` full iff
`p ≤ z ∈ Z(A)` implies `z = 1` (in `vNᵒᵖ`: central carrier `⌈⌈p⌉⌉ = 1`).

**(D1) common full corners.** `∃ C`, `p,q ∈ SPred(C)` full, `A ≅ {C|p}`, `B ≅ {C|q}`;
`~` the equivalence relation this generates. Equivalently: `A`, `B` are joined by
a zigzag of *sharp total maps with full support* `{C|p} → C` (comprehensions of full
sharp predicates; in `vNᵒᵖ` the nmiu isos `𝒜 ≅ p𝒞p`, exa-sharp-vn).

**(D2) stable isomorphism.** Call `I` *Morita-trivial* if `1 ≅ {I|p}` for a full `p`
(`vNᵒᵖ`: `pIp = ℂ`, `⌈⌈p⌉⌉ = 1`, i.e. `I = B(H)`). `A ~ B` iff `A ⊗ I ≅ B ⊗ J` with
`I, J` Morita-trivial. Transitive if `{I⊗J | p⊗q} ≅ {I|p} ⊗ {J|q}` and full⊗full is
full (both hold in `vN`; propose them as axioms for a "monoidal †-effectus with
comprehension").

**(D3) comprehension categories.** `Cmp(A)` := full subcategory of `Pure C` on the
`{A|p}`, `p ∈ SPred(A)`; `A ~ B` iff `Cmp(A) ≃ Cmp(B)`.

**S5.** In `vNᵒᵖ`: (D1) and (D2) both coincide with Rieffel's W*-Morita equivalence
(`𝒜 ≅ p(ℬ ⊗̄ B(H))p`, `p` full ⟺ imprimitivity self-dual bimodule ⟺ stably
isomorphic); (D3) does **not** — it is nmiu-isomorphism. *Sketch.* (D1) ⇐ Morita:
the linking algebra `L = ℬᵃ(X ⊕ ℬ)` of an imprimitivity bimodule has `e₁, e₂` full
(X full on both sides) with corners `𝒜, ℬ` — one step suffices. (D1) ⇒ Morita:
`p𝒞p ~ 𝒞 ~ q𝒞q` for full `p, q` (Rieffel). (D2): `𝒜 ⊗̄ B(H) ≅ ℬ ⊗̄ B(K)` is Rieffel's
stable-isomorphism form; for the converse `p ⊗ 1_{H'} ~ 1 ⊗ 1_{H'}` for `dim H'`
large by `1 ≼ p ⊗ 1_{H'}` (from `⌈⌈p⌉⌉ = 1`) and Schröder–Bernstein. (D3) fails:
`Cmp(ℂ) ≃ {0, ℂ}` but `Cmp(M₂) ≃ {0, ℂ, M₂}` and `M₂ ≇ ℂ` in `Pure`; more generally
`A = {A|1}` is the weakly terminal object of `Cmp(A)` (every object has a
comprehension into it), an equivalence preserves it, so `Cmp(A) ≃ Cmp(B) ⇒ A ≅ B`.
Refutation risk: the "full" notion depends on the centre note being right; low.

**S6 (`Kl(𝒟)`).** All sharp predicates are central, so full = `1`, Morita-trivial
= `1`, and (D1) = (D2) = isomorphism: `X ~ Y` iff `|X| = |Y|` — as for commutative
rings and for commutative algebras in `vNᵒᵖ`.

**S7 (`EJAᵒᵖ`, finite-dimensional).** No tensor, so only (D1). Corners are Peirce
1-spaces `U_pE`. Every simple EJA has a minimal idempotent `p` with `U_pE = ℝ`,
and `p` is full (simple ⇒ centre trivial). Hence every simple EJA is `~ ℝ`, and
`E ~ F` iff `Z(E) ≅ Z(F)` iff same number of simple summands. **Morita sees
neither the Jordan type (`ℍ_n(ℝ), ℍ_n(ℂ), ℍ_n(ℍ), ℍ₃(𝕆)`, spin) nor the state
space — only the centre.** Not a defect of EJA: finite-dimensional `vNᵒᵖ` collapses
the same way (all `M_n ~ ℂ`); II₁-type content needs JBW-algebras. **Best
definition: (D1)** (no tensor needed, recovers Rieffel); (D2) where `⊗` exists.

## (iii) Morita-closure: one step vs a chain

**S8 (`vNᵒᵖ` is one-step closed).** For full sharp `p` on `𝒜`, `{𝒜|p} ~ 𝒜` (take
`C = 𝒜`, `q = 1`) — trivial under (D1). The real content: *the one-step relation of
(D1) is already an equivalence relation in `vNᵒᵖ`.* Given `𝒜 = p𝒞p`, `ℬ = q𝒞q` and
`ℬ = q'𝒞'q'`, `𝒟 = r𝒞'r` (all full), the fusion `𝒞q ⊗_ℬ q'𝒞'` is an `𝒜`–`𝒟`
imprimitivity bimodule (fusion of invertible 1-cells; `𝒞q` restricted to `p𝒞p` is
invertible since `p, q` full), and its linking algebra is the common `C''`.
Effectus-internally this is a *cospan-composition* property for full
comprehensions, and it needs the interior tensor — it is not derivable from the
†-effectus axioms alone (see S9).

**S9 (`EJAᵒᵖ` is not one-step closed — refutation of naive Morita-closure).**
`ℍ₂(ℝ) ≅ V₂ ~ ℝ ~ V₃ ≅ ℍ₂(ℂ)` (S7), but no EJA `G` has both as full corners:
`ℍ₂(ℝ)` simple and `p` full force `G` simple; corners of `ℍ_n(𝕂)` are `ℍ_k(𝕂)`
(same `𝕂`), of spin factors `ℝ` or the factor itself, of `ℍ₃(𝕆)` are `ℝ, V₉, ℍ₃(𝕆)`.
So `V₂` and `V₃` are never full corners of one simple `G`. Hence: in `EJAᵒᵖ` the
generated relation (D1) is strictly coarser than its one-step version, i.e. the
2×2 linking-algebra picture is a genuinely `vN` phenomenon (Jordan algebras have no
linking algebra because `X ⊗_ℬ Y` is not a Jordan bimodule construction).
Checkable by hand; risk low.

**S10 (reconstructing the bicategory inside the effectus).** In `vNᵒᵖ` a 1-cell
`𝒜 ⇸ ℬ` is, up to iso, a triple `(L, e, ρ)`: object `L`, sharp `e` with `{L|e} ≅ ℬ`
and `⌈⌈e⌉⌉ = 1`, and a sharp total map `ρ : {L|e^⊥} → 𝒜` in `vNᵒᵖ` (i.e. nmiu
`𝒜 → (1−e)L(1−e) = ℬᵃ((1−e)Le)`, the S4 remark of `modules-as-corners.md` gives
`ℬᵃ` from `⌈⌈e⌉⌉ = 1`). 2-cells `(L,e,ρ) → (L',e',ρ')`: 2-cells between corners of
one algebra are elements `t ∈ (1−e)L(1−e)` intertwining ρ, so first amalgamate
`L, L'` over `ℬ` (again the fusion/linking construction). Objects, 1-cells and
identity 1-cells (`(M₂(𝒜), e₁₁, id)`) are effectus-definable; *composition is not
definable from the †-effectus structure alone* — it is exactly the extra datum that
S8 needs and S9 shows is absent in `EJAᵒᵖ`. Proposal: a "Morita †-effectus" is a
†-effectus with a chosen cospan-composition of full comprehensions satisfying the
bicategory coherences; `vNᵒᵖ` is one (via fusion), `EJAᵒᵖ` is not, `Kl(𝒟)` trivially.
Refutation risk: moderate — the coherence axioms for `(L,e,ρ)`-composition are not
written down here.

## (iv) Picard groups

`Pic(𝒜) := Aut_{W*Corr}(𝒜)` = invertible self-dual `𝒜`–`𝒜` correspondences under
fusion (by definition, the autoequivalences of the object in the bicategory).
Known: `Pic(B(H)) = 1`; type III factor `Pic(M) = Out(M)`; II₁ factor
`1 → Out(M) → Pic(M) → 𝓕(M) → 1` with `𝓕(M)` the fundamental group;
commutative `Pic(L^∞) = Aut(L^∞)`.

**S11 (what the effectus sees: `Aut`, and `Inn` via the qubit).** Automorphisms of
the object `𝒜` in `vNᵒᵖ` and in `Pure(vNᵒᵖ)` are the nmiu automorphisms `Aut(𝒜)`
(dagger-of-iso: `α^† = α^{-1}`), and `θ ↦ {}_θ𝒜` is a homomorphism `Aut(𝒜) → Pic(𝒜)`
with kernel `Inn(𝒜) = {ad_u}` (`{}_θ𝒜 ≅ 𝒜` iff θ inner). `Inn` is effectus-definable:
let `Q` be the qubit object (dilation of `⟨½,½⟩`, eff 6949/absdilsdag, `Q = M₂` in
`vNᵒᵖ`) with its sharp atom `e`; then `θ` is inner iff there is an isomorphism `Θ`
of `𝒜 ⊗ Q` with `Θ(1 ⊗ e) = 1 ⊗ e` restricting to `θ` on `{𝒜⊗Q | 1⊗e}` and to `id`
on `{𝒜⊗Q | 1⊗e^⊥}`. *Sketch.* ⇐: `w := Θ(e₁₂) ∈ 𝒜e₁₂` is `u e₁₂` with `u`
unitary and `Θ(a e₁₂) = θ(a) e₁₁ w = w a e₂₂` forces `θ(a)u = ua`. ⇒: `Θ = ad_{diag(u,1)}`.
So `Out(𝒜) = Aut(𝒜)/Inn(𝒜)` is a †-effectus invariant and `Out(𝒜) ↪ Pic(𝒜)`.
**Pic does not act on the object `𝒜` of the effectus** — an invertible `X` acts on
the W*-category `W*Corr(ℬ, 𝒜)` (hence on Paschke dilation *objects* `(𝒫, ρ)`, up to
iso trivially since `ℬᵃ(E ⊗ X) ≅ ℬᵃ(E)`), but not on ncp maps, because it forgets
the point `e_φ`. `ad_u` is exactly the part of `Aut` that is invisible in the
bicategory, and the effectus can tell it apart from the rest (S11).

**S12 (the rest of `Pic` is the (iii) relation `{p} ~ A`).** In `vNᵒᵖ` every
invertible `X : 𝒜 ⇸ 𝒜` is a corner `(1−e)Le` with `{L|e} ≅ 𝒜 ≅ {L|e^⊥}` and both
`e, e^⊥` full; conversely each such "self-Morita context" is invertible. So
`Pic(𝒜) ≅ {(L, e, α, β) : e, e^⊥ full, α : 𝒜 ≅ {L|e}, β : 𝒜 ≅ {L|e^⊥}} / ≅`
as a *set*, effectus-internally; the group law is fusion (S10's missing datum),
inverses are `e ↔ e^⊥`. For a II₁ factor: `(L,e) ~ (p, θ : M ≅ pMp)`, `p` full, and
the class in `𝓕(M)` is `τ(p)` where `τ` is the unique inner-automorphism-invariant
state (definable by S11 given scalars). Test cases: `𝒜 = B(H)`: any such `L` is
`B(H ⊕ H)`-like, so `Pic = 1` ✓. `Kl(𝒟)`: no `(L,e)` with `e, e^⊥` both full exists
unless `L = ∅`, so the corner description gives `Pic(X) = 1`, contradicting
`Pic = Aut = Sym(X)` for commutative objects — the description needs the qubit
(`M₂(𝒜)` is the linking algebra of `{}_θ𝒜`, and `Kl(𝒟)` has no Morita-trivial
object besides `1`). So S12 is `vNᵒᵖ`-specific; the general definition should be
"automorphisms in the reconstructed bicategory of S10", not "self-Morita contexts".
Refutation risk of S12 in `vNᵒᵖ`: low (it is Rieffel's linking-algebra theorem
specialised to `𝒜 = ℬ`); of the `𝓕(M)` clause: moderate (needs `τ(p)` in `[0,1]`
to be read off from the effectus scalars — true since scalars of `vNᵒᵖ` are `[0,1]`).

## Ranking

1. S1–S4: Paschke is `End` of the initial pointed 1-cell and a colax functor into
   `W*Corr` (iso iff `e_φ ⊗ e_ψ` cyclic; `ℂ → ℬ → ℂ` counterexample); 157IV is the
   2-endomorphism algebra. Cheapest to make precise, most useful to dils.
2. S5 + S9: (D1) recovers Rieffel; one-step closure holds in `vNᵒᵖ` and fails in
   `EJAᵒᵖ` (`ℍ₂(ℝ)` vs `ℍ₂(ℂ)`), pinpointing fusion as the datum a †-effectus lacks.
3. S11: `Inn`, hence `Out ↪ Pic`, is †-effectus-definable via the qubit object.
4. S10/S12: bicategory and `Pic` as `(L,e,ρ)`-data — composition not yet axiomatised.
